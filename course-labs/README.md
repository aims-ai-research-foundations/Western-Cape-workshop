# Course Labs

Notebooks for the **Course Labs**, organised by day. Click an **Open in Colab**
badge to launch a notebook directly in Google Colab — no local setup required.
Times on the workshop timetable are in SAST; the Friday Course 7 lab runs
14:00–15:00, and the Monday to Thursday labs are not timetabled sessions.

Days 1, 2 and 3 are here now; the other days land later. Days 1–4 run on CPU with no
setup; the Day 5 lab needs a Colab **T4 GPU** and access to Gemma 3 1B and 4B —
the setup steps arrive with the notebook in [day5/](day5/).
